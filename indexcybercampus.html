WHY IT EXISTS

Real cyber skills,
disguised as a mystery.

Students are the most phished people online — and awareness slideshows don't stick. CyberCampus makes the lesson a game you actually want to finish.

🔍

Think like an investigator

Read real phishing headers, portal logs and keycard records. Learn what evidence proves — and what it doesn't.

🧠

Concepts that stick

Phishing, compromised accounts, access control, incident reporting — taught through decisions, not bullet points.

⚖️

Consequences you feel

Accuse the wrong person and trust collapses. One rule runs the whole game: a login proves when, never who.
